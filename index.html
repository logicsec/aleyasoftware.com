One team. Every system the building depends on.

Cabling is the part nobody sees and everything else sits on top of. We design and install it alongside the cameras, doors and wireless that hang off it — one crew, one schedule, and one number to call when something needs to change.

The full UniFi stack — designed, installed, supported.

Network, Protect, Access and Talk on one controller, planned against the real floor plan before hardware is ordered. Multi-site management, console health checks, and wireless sized for the devices that will actually be in the room. At handover your team gets the as-built drawings, port maps and credentials to run it without us.

Network design

Topology, VLANs, IP scheme and firewall rules settled on paper first. Greenfield builds, and untangling networks that grew without a plan.

WiFi planning

Predictive coverage, on-site validation, AP placement, channel planning and transmit-power tuning under real load.

Cabling & rack builds

Cat6, Cat6A and OM4 risers. Labeled patch panels, certified with a tester and photo-documented at handoff.

Security cameras

Camera and lens selection, NVR sizing, motion-zone tuning and real coverage maps. Local recording, no monthly fee.

Access control

Card, fob, mobile credential and video intercom. Door schedules, anti-passback, audit logs and free egress on every opening.

Custom software

Internal tools, dashboards and integrations between the systems you already run — when off-the-shelf will not fit the brief.

Network diagnostics

A day on site auditing topology, surveying RF and reviewing config, ending with a straight answer on what is wrong and what to do.

Managed services

Console health checks, firmware windows, verified backups and on-call for incidents. Priced per device.

Built once, documented, and handed over.

You have a building, a deadline, and nobody who owns the low-voltage yet. We take it from an empty floor plan to a system your own team can run — designed before anything is ordered, installed on one schedule, and supported long after we drive away.

01

Design

We walk the building, take real signal readings and check every pathway, then hand back a full design before a single part is ordered — device placement, cable schedule, port map, VLAN layout and an itemized bill of materials.

  • Coverage surveyed on site · placement fixed before purchase
  • Written bill of materials · every part itemized
  • Scope and schedule agreed · before we drill a hole
02

Deploy

One crew runs the cabling, racks, network, cameras and doors on a single schedule, so nothing sits waiting on another trade. When we leave, your team knows what went in, how it is connected, and who can safely touch it next.

  • Every copper run certified · tester report included
  • Labeled both ends · matching the as-built drawings
  • Racks dressed and photographed · site clean daily
03

Defend

Optional after-care once it is live. Console health checks, scheduled firmware windows, verified configuration backups, and a number that reaches someone who already knows your site.

  • Credentials and documentation · handed over, not held
  • Firmware windows · verified configuration backups
  • On-call for incidents · priced per device
